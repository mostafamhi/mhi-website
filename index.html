What Are Fire Alarm Systems?

A Fire Alarm System is a critical life-safety network of devices working in concert to detect, alert, and protect against the threat of fire — enabling rapid response before disaster strikes.

Fyreye MKII Optical Smoke Detector

The Zeta Fyreye MKII is an addressable optical smoke detector with built-in isolator — EN54-7 certified and engineered for maximum reliability in critical environments. It forms the backbone of every fire detection system MHI designs and installs.

Early Detection

Smoke detectors, heat sensors, and flame detectors identify fire at its earliest stage — before it becomes uncontrollable — giving occupants vital evacuation time.

Automatic & Manual Alerts

Integrated call points and automatic triggering activate sounders, strobes, and control panels simultaneously, ensuring no one is unaware of a developing fire emergency.

Visual & Audio Appliances

High-decibel sounders combined with bright visual strobes alert occupants across large and complex buildings — critical for accessibility and noisy environments.

Central Monitoring

Addressable control panels provide real-time status of every device, pinpoint alarm locations instantly, and integrate with building management and suppression systems.

System Integration

Modern FAS links with HVAC, access control, elevator recall, and automatic suppression systems to create a fully coordinated emergency response.

Ongoing Maintenance

Regular inspection, testing, and maintenance ensures your system remains fully operational — meeting regulatory standards and insurance requirements at all times.

Why Proper FAS Installation Matters

An incorrectly installed or unmaintained fire alarm system can mean the difference between a controlled evacuation and a catastrophic loss. Trust only certified specialists.
